Late-Night Texts and Real Feelings

Posted on October 21,2026 by James Mason
All Posts

Have you ever typed out a text message that was a little too honest, got scared, and hit delete? Brent Faiyaz, Jhené Aiko, and Drake don't erase those messages—they sing them over a beat. Over the last ten years, these three music artists changed the game for modern R&B and Hip-Hop.

Instead of writing fancy poetry or classic love stories, they write simple, direct lyrics about real life. Their songs feel like reading someone’s private notes app, complete with late-night thoughts, big egos, and messy relationship drama.

Writing Like You Talk

All three artists write lyrics that sound like real conversations or private journal entries. They avoid fake, overly poetic lyrics and just speak the truth.

  • Drake names real ex-girlfriends, specific cities, and exact times of day in his lyrics to make them feel personal.
  • Jhené Aiko writes like she is opening her diary, sharing honest feelings about loss, healing, and heartbreak.
  • Brent Faiyaz uses plain language to show the messy truth about fame and casual dating.

"Toxic" Love and Flawed Characters

Instead of writing perfect, happy love songs, these writers are not afraid to show their bad side. They openly write about being jealous, self-centered, and petty.

  • Brent Faiyaz is often called a "toxic" singer because his lyrics put his own lifestyle and independence above long-term relationship promises.
  • Drake made it cool to be both sensitive and arrogant at the same time—switching from missing his ex to showing off his wealth.
  • Jhené Aiko brings a cooler side to the table, writing about moving on, tuning out drama, or giving a partner that same cold shoulder (like in her famous song "The Worst").

Mixing Singing and Rapping

These artists do not treat Hip-Hop and R&B like two different things. They mix them together. They write melodies using a rapper's fast rhythm, and they rap with a singer's smooth voice. Because their styles fit together so well, they have made great songs together—like Drake featuring Jhené Aiko on "From Time" and Brent Faiyaz featuring Drake on "Wasting Time".

Quiet Beats and Soft Voices

Their songwriting relies on negative space—which means leaving room to breathe. They set their lyrics over quiet, slow, and deep bass beats. Because the music is simple, they don't have to shout. Their quiet, half-spoken singing style makes the songs feel like a secret shared just between the singer and you.

Key Songs and Themes Compared
